# Executive Board Meeting Minutes – Alpstein Robotics AG

**Date:** 12 March 2026, 08:30–10:15
**Place:** Appenzell, Säntis meeting room
**Present:** Dr. Lea Brunner (CEO, chair), Marco Steiner (CFO), Priya Raman (CTO), Jonas Weber (Head of Service), Sandra Koller (Head of Sales)
**Minutes:** Executive Board assistant

---

## 1. Business performance January–February

Marco Steiner reports: Order intake is 12% above the previous year. The driver is AlpPick among logistics customers. Service revenue (AlpCare) is growing more slowly than planned. Alpstein Robotics currently employs **120 employees** at the sites in Appenzell and Buchs SG.

**Decision:** No adjustment to the annual budget. Next review in June.

## 2. Product: AlpPick 2.0

Priya Raman presents the current status. The new generation of the picking robot has a higher payload (25 kg instead of 15 kg) and new navigation without floor markings. Two pilot robots have been running at Bergland Logistik AG in Buchs since February.

Open points: Battery life in the cold store (target 8 hours, currently 6.5 hours). CE certification is in progress.

**Decision:** **Market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in September 2026.** Sandra Koller prepares the customer communication by the end of May.

## 3. Service: AlpCare

Jonas Weber: Remote monitoring via AlpMind reduces on-site visits by about 30%. Customers are asking for a variant with a guaranteed response time of under 4 hours. The proposal "AlpCare Plus" will be presented in May with the strategy memo.

Current AlpCare price: CHF 1,200 per robot and month.

**Decision:** Jonas Weber and Marco Steiner develop a pricing model for AlpCare Plus by the May meeting.

## 4. Personnel

Lea Brunner: Recruitment for software development is under way. Six positions are open, four of them in Buchs. The onboarding process is to be digitized by summer.

## 5. Other business

- Team event in September, organized by the Executive Board assistant.
- Next Executive Board meeting: 7 May 2026.
