# Strategy Memo: Service first – Alpstein Robotics AG 2026–2028

**From:** Dr. Lea Brunner, CEO
**To:** Executive Board, Board of Directors
**Date:** 5 May 2026
**Status:** Draft for discussion at the Executive Board meeting of 7 May 2026

---

## Starting point

We sell robots. But our customers buy something else: they buy the assurance that their warehouse keeps running. The hardware market for picking robots is getting tighter; three new providers from Germany and Poland entered Switzerland in 2025. Our edge is not the robot but the operation: AlpMind (fleet software) and AlpCare (service).

With our **140 employees**, we are big enough to scale service and small enough to be fast.

## Goal

By 2028, service revenue should make up **50% of total revenue** (today: about one third). We are moving from robot manufacturer to operator partner.

## Three initiatives

### 1. AlpCare Plus

A service subscription with a guaranteed response time of under 4 hours, remote monitoring around the clock and a replacement robot within 24 hours. Target customers: pharma and hospitals, where downtime is expensive.

Price: AlpCare stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month**. AlpCare Plus is calculated at **CHF 1,900**. Responsible: Jonas Weber.

### 2. AlpMind as a platform

AlpMind should also be able to control robots from other manufacturers. This makes us the operator of mixed fleets. First interface: the vehicles of our competitor from Bavaria, which are already in use at Rheintal Pharma AG. Responsible: Priya Raman.

### 3. Agentic AI in service

An AI agent should classify service tickets automatically, resolve standard cases directly and hand complex cases over to humans. Goal: a response time of under 10 minutes in the standard case. Pilot from August 2026. Responsible: Jonas Weber with Priya Raman.

## Risks

- **Loss of focus:** If AlpPick 2.0 arrives in the fall, it pulls attention away from service.
- **Price pressure:** Customers compare AlpCare with pure maintenance contracts, which are significantly cheaper.
- **Liability for AI:** If the agent makes a wrong commitment in service, we are liable. We need clear rules for what the agent may do.
- **Personnel:** Service technicians are hard to find. Remote monitoring must make up for the shortage.

## Decisions needed at the Executive Board meeting

1. Approval of the target of 50% service revenue by 2028.
2. Approval of the pricing model AlpCare Plus.
3. Approval of the pilot budget for Agentic AI (CHF 180,000).
