# Quarterly Report Q2 2026 – Excerpt for the Executive Board

**Alpstein Robotics AG** · Reporting period 1 April – 30 June 2026 · Prepared by: Finance (Marco Steiner) · Date: 30 July 2026 · Confidentiality level: internal (fictional practice document)

## 1. Summary

In the second quarter, revenue was slightly above plan and earnings were slightly below. The reasons are higher service costs from three downtime incidents at Bergland Logistik AG and the earlier-than-planned recruitment in software development. The service share of revenue continues to rise. Headcount on 30 June is **140 employees** (full-time equivalents: 131).

## 3. Commentary by area

**Hardware.** 34 new AlpPick delivered, 18 of them to Bergland Logistik AG (expansion of Halls 3 and 4) and 9 to Toggenburg Möbel AG (new customer). Demand for AlpPick 2.0 is high; 41 pre-orders are in. The launch date will be set at the Executive Board meeting in August.

**Software.** AlpMind licenses grow with the fleet. The interface to third-party robots (initiative "AlpMind as a platform") is at prototype stage; the first test at Rheintal Pharma AG is planned for Q3.

**Service.** AlpCare contracts are growing faster than the fleet. Three downtime incidents at Bergland (May/June) led to special costs of CHF 62,000 (call-outs, spare parts). A credit note to Bergland has **not** been booked so far; the decision lies with the Executive Board. The pricing model for AlpCare Plus (CHF 1,900) is approved; the AlpCare price list (CHF 1,200) remains unchanged.

**Personnel.** 12 new hires, 8 of them in software development. The open positions in service field staff (4) are still unfilled.

## 4. Risks and outlook

1. **Delivery date AlpPick 2.0:** A postponement to Q4 would endanger the pre-orders; Bergland has planned an expansion for September.
2. **Service costs:** The navigation error has been fixed since the patch of 24 June. Further downtime would weigh on the margin.
3. **Agentic AI pilot:** Budget of CHF 180,000 approved; kickoff in August. The risk lies less in the technology than in the rules: What may the agent commit to toward customers?

The full-year outlook remains at CHF 38 million revenue and a 10% EBIT margin.
