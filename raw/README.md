# raw/ – Your sources

The originals live here. The agent reads them and never changes them.

## Sample documents: Alpstein Robotics AG (fictional)

The company, people, numbers and customers in `raw/alpstein/` are invented. They are for practice only. The documents deliberately contain:

- an **outdated statement** (a figure that changes over time),
- a **contradiction** (two sources, two different statements, no explanation),
- an **ambiguous decision** (the text does not make clear what was decided),
- an **irrelevant document** (it does not belong in a knowledge wiki).

Your agent should find all of this. Whether it does depends on your `CLAUDE.md`.
