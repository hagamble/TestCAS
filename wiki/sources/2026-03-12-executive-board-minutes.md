# Executive Board minutes, 12 March 2026

## Summary

The Executive Board of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] met on 12 March 2026 in Appenzell. Order intake for January–February is 12% above the previous year, driven by [[alppick]]; service revenue from [[alpcare]] grows more slowly than planned. The board decided to launch AlpPick 2.0 in September 2026 ([[alppick-2-0-launch]]) and to develop a pricing model for AlpCare Plus ([[alpcare-plus]]). Recruitment is under way and the onboarding process is to be digitized ([[digital-onboarding]]). The annual budget stays unchanged, with the next review in June.

## Key points

- Order intake Jan–Feb is 12% above the previous year; driver is AlpPick among logistics customers. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service revenue (AlpCare) is growing more slowly than planned. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- 120 employees at the sites Appenzell and Buchs SG. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Decision: no adjustment to the annual budget; next review in June. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Decision: market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in September 2026. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Remote monitoring via [[alpmind]] reduces on-site visits by about 30%. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Current AlpCare price: CHF 1,200 per robot and month. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Six software development positions are open, four of them in Buchs. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Other business: team event in September; next Executive Board meeting 7 May 2026. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
